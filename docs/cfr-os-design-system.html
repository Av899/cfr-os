CFR OS design system

One visual language for every screen in the café: the register, the kitchen display, the server pagers, the back office and the guest menu. Every colour, size and sentence below is meant to be copied straight into the Next.js, Tailwind and shadcn/ui build.

Drag the venue clock to move the whole page from the morning workspace to the lounge after the 6 PM lighting change. Every live component on this page follows it, exactly as the register will.

Principles

CFR OS carries a printed-menu café into software. These four rules settle most design arguments.

Paper first
Screens behave like a printed card: a warm ground, hairline borders and almost no shadow. If a surface needs a drop shadow to read, the layout is wrong.
Quiet until it matters
Beyond the five brand tones, colour appears only to report status: new, preparing, ready, late, offline. Nothing else is allowed to shout.
Hands are busy
Targets are 44 px on the guest menu, 56 px on the register and 64 px in the kitchen. Nothing a barista taps with a wet thumb is smaller.
Offline is normal
Every operational surface shows its sync state and keeps working without the network. Failures say what is saved and what happens next.

Rules

Item names use Title Case exactly as printed on the menu. All interface text uses sentence case. Prices, quantities and timers use tabular figures so columns never jitter when a socket update lands. The ₹ sign sits directly before the amount with no space. Tracked capitals belong to the wordmark subtitle and the roman section numerals only.

The font link at the top of this file loads Montserrat from Google Fonts for this preview. In the build, self-host it with next/font so the register works with no network.

Tokens and code

Drop these into the project as they are. The theme variables use shadcn/ui's role names, so every shadcn component picks up the brand without edits. Theme switching follows the venue clock: dark from 6 PM, light from 6 AM.

On Tailwind v4, move the same values into an @theme block and wrap each one in hsl(). A useVenueTheme() hook should call venueTheme() every minute and toggle the dark class on <html>. The kitchen display and pagers pin it to dark.

Money and tax

Menu prices are base prices in ₹10 steps, shown exclusive of 5% GST (2.5% CGST plus 2.5% SGST). Because every price and add-on is a multiple of ₹10, each 2.5% share is always a multiple of ₹0.25, so CGST and SGST are exact to the paisa with no rounding on a normal bill.

Rules

Store money as integer paise and format it only at the edge. Calculate GST once on the bill subtotal, never per line. Format with Indian grouping: ₹1,23,456. The menu shows no decimals; the bill and receipt always show two.

Guests never see GST-inclusive prices. The menu footer carries "Prices Excl. 5% GST", and the bill shows the split.

Confirm round-off and invoice numbering rules with your CA before launch. This system only fixes how they look.

Voice

Accessibility and motion

  

    

      
Accessibility

      
All text meets 4.5:1 against its ground, as the contrast table shows. Latte is never text on Cream. Focus shows a 2 px ring with a 2 px offset: Sage by day, Latte at night. Status always pairs colour with a word. Every control is a real button, link or input with a visible label, and icon-only buttons carry an aria-label.

    

    

      
Motion

      
Three durations: 120 ms for press feedback, 200 ms for opening and closing, 320 ms for the day-to-night change. Easing is cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1). When a socket update changes a ticket, flash its background to the Latte wash once, for 600 ms, then settle. Nothing loops. With reduced motion on, transitions are removed and the flash becomes a static Latte border.
